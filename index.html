About

Research-oriented LLM solutions architect

I am an AI/NLP researcher, LLM solutions architect, entrepreneur, and technical educator based in Seoul, South Korea. My work focuses on domain-specific large language model applications, retrieval-augmented generation, private LLM systems, document intelligence, embedding-based retrieval, parameter-efficient fine-tuning, text mining, and responsible AI evaluation.

I am the founder of HappyAI, an AI and big data analytics company that develops LLM, RAG, NLP, text mining, and document intelligence solutions for public institutions, enterprises, universities, and research organizations. I have led and participated in more than 50 AI, NLP, big data, and text mining projects across public policy, healthcare, education, legal documents, infrastructure, software industry analysis, forest science, consumer analytics, and media perception analysis.

I received my Ph.D. in Artificial Intelligence from Dongguk University. My doctoral research developed a structure-aware bias evaluation and mitigation framework for transformer-based language models, comparing encoder-based BERT classification models and decoder-based GPT-series generative models — connecting AI fairness research with practical LLM evaluation and governance workflows for enterprise and public-sector AI systems.

I also teach practical AI software, NLP, LLMs, RAG, prompt engineering, fine-tuning, and AI application development as an adjunct professor and professional AI instructor. My teaching experience includes enterprise and public-sector AI training for organizations such as Samsung SDS, KT, Seoul Digital Foundation, Inflearn, Letuin Edu, Kyonggi University, and the Korea Vocational Development Institute.

My goal is to bridge frontier AI research and practical AI deployment by building reliable, domain-specific AI systems that can be used in real organizational environments.
